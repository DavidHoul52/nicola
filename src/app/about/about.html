About me

An experienced psychological therapist offering individual CBT and couples therapy.

My Story

Before becoming a psychological therapist, I worked as a specialist neurological speech and language therapist, supporting people living with long-term and life-changing conditions. This gave me a particular understanding of how difficulties can affect the whole person — including relationships, identity and our sense of what is possible.

My speech and language therapy background also taught me how to adapt to different processing and learning styles. Having experienced therapy myself, I understand that it can feel daunting, and I’ll do my best to help you feel at ease.

I later completed additional NHS training in working with neurodiversity and long-term health conditions.

In all, I spent 23 years working in the NHS before setting up my private practice, where I continue to enjoy getting to know and working with each person I see.

My approach

A particular interest of mine is the way our emotional lives and our relationships shape each other. In CBT we look closely at the patterns that keep difficulties going in the present, and make sense of how they first developed. From there, we work on new ways of responding — to yourself, to the people around you and to life more broadly — so you can meet things with more kindness, confidence and flexibility.

Depending on your needs, I may also draw on mindfulness, Compassion Focused Therapy (CFT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT).

My style

I’m warm, curious and down-to-earth, and use humour when it feels appropriate. I take my work seriously, but I don’t believe therapy needs to feel overly formal or clinical. I bring considerable experience and knowledge, while remaining curious about each person and their circumstances.

Qualifications and experience

My core training is in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, the approach I use with individuals. To practise CBT, I completed a Postgraduate Diploma at the Charlie Waller Institute, University of Reading.

I have additional integrative training in couples therapy — Couple Therapy for Depression — which I obtained through Tavistock Relationships.

As a BABCP-accredited therapist, I have demonstrated and continue to uphold the professional standards required in CBT training, clinical experience, therapeutic practice, clinical supervision and ongoing professional development. This accreditation reflects my commitment to delivering safe, effective and high-quality care to my clients.

I am also an approved Couple Therapy for Depression practitioner with Tavistock Relationships.
