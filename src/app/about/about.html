About me

An experienced psychological therapist offering individual CBT and couples therapy.

Hello, I’m Nicola

I’m an experienced BABCP-accredited psychological therapist, specialising in individual CBT and couples therapy. I trained at Tavistock Relate, using an integrative approach.

After 23 years in the NHS, I developed a private practice because I genuinely enjoy working with people. Before becoming a therapist, I trained as a neurological specialist speech and language therapist, and have extensive experience supporting people with a wide range of psychological and health-related difficulties.

My approach

I’m particularly interested in how emotional difficulties and relationship patterns interact. Using CBT, we explore the patterns that may be causing difficulties now, while also understanding where they came from. Together, we can try new ways of relating to yourself, others and the world — with greater kindness, confidence and flexibility.

My style

I’m warm, curious and down-to-earth, and use humour when it feels appropriate. I take my work seriously, but I don’t believe therapy needs to feel overly formal or clinical. I bring considerable experience and knowledge, while remaining curious about each person and their circumstances.
