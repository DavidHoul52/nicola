My experience

My core training is in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), which I use in my work with individuals. I completed a Postgraduate Diploma in CBT at the Charlie Waller Institute, University of Reading.

I have also undertaken specialist integrative training in couples therapy at Tavistock Relationships, and am an approved Couple Therapy for Depression practitioner.

I spent 23 years working in the NHS before setting up my private practice. My background includes working as a psychological therapist and, before that, as a specialist neurological speech and language therapist, supporting people living with long-term and life-changing conditions. I bring extensive clinical experience of supporting people with a wide range of psychological and health-related difficulties.

Through this work, I’ve seen how life’s difficulties can affect the whole person, including their relationships, identity and sense of what’s possible.

I continue to learn and read widely, drawing on ideas from different therapeutic traditions. I’m interested in what helps people make lasting changes, develop greater self-understanding and compassion, and build relationships that feel more connected and workable.

About the BABCP

As a BABCP-accredited therapist, I have demonstrated and continue to uphold the professional standards required in CBT training, clinical experience, therapeutic practice, clinical supervision and ongoing professional development. This accreditation reflects my commitment to delivering safe, effective and high-quality care to my clients.
