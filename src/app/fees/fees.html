Where therapy takes place

Therapy is primarily offered face-to-face, with online sessions available where appropriate and by arrangement. Face-to-face therapy offers a dedicated, private space away from everyday distractions, allowing for a more personal therapeutic connection. Being in the same room can also support greater awareness of non-verbal communication, helping to build rapport and create a safe, focused environment for therapeutic work.

In person at Wokingham Therapy Clinic.

How often we meet

This depends on the challenges you’re presenting with and will be discussed at the beginning of therapy and reviewed based on progress as we go. CBT is time limited and is typically up to a maximum of 20 weeks initially, some issues can be worked with in fewer sessions. Towards the end there’s the option of a consolidation period of less frequent sessions.

Payment

Payment is due at the end of each session and can be made conveniently by debit or credit card, either in person or via a secure payment link.

Cancellations

I kindly ask for at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or rearrange an appointment. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice, or missed appointments, will be charged at the full session fee.

Confidentiality

What you share in therapy stays confidential. The rare exceptions — such as a serious risk to you or someone else — are explained in our first session.

How your personal information is stored and used is set out in the privacy notice.
