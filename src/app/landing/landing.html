About me

I'm an experienced psychological therapist accredited by the British Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Psychotherapies (BABCP ) specialising in evidence based Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and couples therapy. I completed a postgraduate diploma in CBT at the University of Reading and then additional training course at Tavistock Relationships to work with couples using an ‘integrative approach’.

In all, I spent 23 years in the NHS, before setting up my small private practice, where I continue to enjoy getting to know and working with each person I see.

Before become a psychological therapist I worked as a specialist neurological speech and language therapist with people living with long term and life changing conditions.

I witnessed how difficulties in life can affect the whole person including relationships, identity and our sense of what’s possible. I have extensive experience supporting people with a wide range of psychological and health related difficulties.

Having experienced therapy myself, I understand that it can feel daunting, and I’ll do my best to help you feel at ease.

My aim is for therapy to be a space where you can feel understood, think about things differently and explore possibilities for change — with someone who brings both professional expertise and genuine human curiosity.

What therapy looks like

The ways we think, feel, cope and relate to others are often shaped by our earlier experiences and the relationships we've had along the way. Many of the patterns we develop make sense in the context of our past and may once have helped us feel safe or cope with difficult situations. But sometimes these ways of being no longer serve us so well. Therapy can help us make sense of these patterns, understand ourselves better and gradually find different ways of relating to ourselves and others.

Together, we can explore where these patterns come from, develop a better understanding of ourselves, and begin to let go of what isn't working. Therapy offers a space to experiment with new ways of thinking, feeling and relating to ourselves and others, with greater kindness, confidence and flexibility.
