About me

I’m an experienced psychological therapist, accredited by the British Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Psychotherapies (BABCP), specialising in evidence-based Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and couples therapy. I completed a postgraduate diploma in CBT at the University of Reading, followed by further training at Tavistock Relationships, to work with couples using an ‘integrative’ approach.

My aim is to offer a space where you feel understood, can think about things differently and explore possibilities for change, supported by professional expertise, genuine curiosity and human understanding.

I’m warm and down-to-earth, and enjoy using humour when appropriate. I believe therapy can be thoughtful and meaningful without feeling overly formal or clinical.

I welcome and value people of all backgrounds, identities, cultures and experiences, recognising and respecting what makes each person and relationship unique.

Having experienced therapy myself, I understand that starting therapy can feel daunting, and I’ll do my best to help you feel at ease.

What therapy looks like

The ways we think, feel, cope and relate to others are often shaped by our earlier experiences and relationships. Many of the patterns we develop make sense in the context of our past and may once have helped us feel safe or cope with difficult situations. However, they may not always continue to serve us well.

Together, we can explore where these patterns come from, develop a better understanding of ourselves and begin to let go of what isn’t working. Therapy offers a space to explore new ways of thinking, feeling and relating to ourselves and others, with greater kindness, confidence and flexibility.

  • Understand patterns
  • Build new skills
  • Strengthen relationships
  • Create lasting change
